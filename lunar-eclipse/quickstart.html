Quick start

The whole lunar eclipse workflow, from choosing an event to checking the saved images.

  1. Set your site. Open Dynamic Target Tracking and check the observer coordinates, elevation and time zone. Choose the site where you will image.
  2. Open the lunar planner. Use Tools ▸ Lunar Eclipse Planner or Ctrl+Shift+L.
  3. Find and select the eclipse. Set Window:, click Find lunar eclipses, then inspect the contacts and Moon visibility. A past event is suitable for rehearsal.
  4. Set the imaging plan. Open Plan preferences.... Enter your full-Moon reference and gain, choose the guiding camera arrangement, HDR capture mode, cadence and file settings. Review the brightness model and exposure ceiling.
  5. Review each phase. Inspect its adaptive exposure range, bracket, interval and guiding. Save your work with Save as draft.
  6. Test the camera and focus. Run System benchmark test... with the intended configuration. Establish focus and practise lunar tracking and guiding with the physical rig.
  7. Rehearse. Click Eclipse plan rehearsal.... Start with the internal simulator, then exercise the SharpCap camera and guiding at realistic timing. Inspect the output.
  8. Approve the plan. After reviewing and rehearsing the settings, use Save as final. Recheck the final version in preflight.
  9. On eclipse night, complete preflight. Click Start eclipse imaging. Verify the selected event, site and real clock; apply camera settings, prepare tracking and guiding, verify focus and exposure, and review every checklist item.
  10. Start and monitor. Start the run from preflight. Watch capture progress and alerts in the console; open the capture folder and check saved images.
Two different meanings of LiveLive view in Plan preferences is a camera mode. Live eclipse in preflight uses the real event clock. You can rehearse either camera mode on a simulated clock.

Continue with Prepare in advance for the full checklist, or Planner basics for the controls.
