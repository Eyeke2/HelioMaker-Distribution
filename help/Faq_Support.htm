How to contact customer support?

Asking for Customer Support

If you encounter issues or have questions about HelioMaker, please navigate to the Contact form to leave a message.

For troubleshooting purposes, you may be asked by customer support to provide additional log files. To do this:

  • Open the HelioMaker log folder by selecting Help → Open Log Folder from the menu.
  • Compress the contents of the log folder into a single archive.
  • Include any relevant log files from PHD2 in your message.

Providing these log files will help to diagnose and resolve your issue more efficiently.
