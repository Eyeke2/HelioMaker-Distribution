Integrating the Best of Two Worlds

If you’ve ever tried creating a time-lapse of solar activity but struggled with your subject drifting out of the frame (perhaps due to imperfect polar alignment), or if you’ve found it challenging to maintain a stable view of a specific solar surface feature, you might assume that you need an expensive dedicated solar guider. Such specialized gear can be both costly and hard to find.

HelioMaker creates a bridge between two well known tools in astronomical imaging. Rather than replacing or duplicating their functionality, it combines the powerful video and image capture features of SharpCap Pro with the newly added solar, lunar, and planetary guiding capabilities of the custom version of PHD2, the most widely used open-source guiding software.

HelioMaker offers a solution by enabling you to use your main imaging telescope and primary planetary camera for both capturing time lapse videos and keeping the target steady at the same time. This makes it well suited for solar, lunar, and planetary imaging. The approach simplifies your setup, reduces costs, and eliminates the need for a separate guide scope or additional solar filters.

The Surface Features Detection and Tracking mode in HelioMaker enables the use of the main imaging telescope at high magnification for both imaging and guiding. This algorithm provides the highest level of guiding precision, eliminating the need for short focal-length guide scopes, which traditionally limited guiding accuracy for solar and planetary imaging. By integrating detection and tracking directly into HelioMaker, users can achieve stable, drift-free time-lapse sequences while capturing fine surface details with greater reliability.

Automated Sequencer

HelioMaker functions as an automated sequencing tool, efficiently managing a series of video or image captures by coordinating with both SharpCap Pro and the custom version of PHD2. This integration ensures precise timing and centralized control over the entire imaging process, from calibration and guiding to capturing. HelioMaker provides a mostly automated and reliable workflow for imaging, especially useful for long sessions that can last for hours.

Alternatively, HelioMaker allows users to manage sequence captures directly within SharpCap Pro, while delegating guiding responsibilities entirely to PHD2. This flexibility accommodates a variety of workflows, empowering users to choose the approach that best suits their imaging needs.

Whether the sequence is initiated in HelioMaker or directly within SharpCap Pro, most of SharpCap Pro's tools remain accessible in its user interface. These include focusing tools, the histogram, the seeing monitor, and other features, allowing you to refine your setup as the capture progresses. Similarly, PHD2 remains fully operational, enabling you to monitor and adjust detection and guiding parameters as needed. This flexibility supports fine-tuning detection and guiding both before and during an active capture session.

This version of HelioMaker introduces a new tool, Find and Track, which enables reliable detection and tracking of all natural celestial bodies, including Saturn, Jupiter, the Sun, the Moon, planets, and stars. While guiding on stars is a traditional feature of PHD2, integrating it with HelioMaker and SharpCap makes it possible to perform guided lucky imaging using a single telescope and camera. This approach simplifies the imaging setup and helps reduce overall costs.

Leveraging the Strength of PHD2

HelioMaker relies on PHD2’s standard guiding capabilities alongside the Advanced Solar and Planetary Detection, recognized as a breakthrough for solar and lunar imaging. These enhancements extend PHD2’s star detection abilities to include solar system targets such as the Sun, the Moon (in full disk, eclipsed, or crescent form), surface features on these bodies, and planets. While other astroimaging tools also offer planetary guiding features, they may not always provide the consistency and reliability required for extended time lapse sessions. HelioMaker, using PHD2’s enhanced detection and guiding capabilities for solar system objects, is designed to deliver stable performance and eliminate drift, enabling you to capture high quality time lapses or static images.

Advanced Solar and Planetary Detection functionality was initially developed and contributed by the HelioMaker author to the custom version of the open-source PHD2 guiding software. To learn more about the custom PHD2 version, please follow this link.

Integration with SharpCap Pro

Integration with SharpCap Pro is a key component of HelioMaker’s functionality. Although HelioMaker and SharpCap Pro are separate programs requiring independent installation, HelioMaker is designed to integrate seamlessly with SharpCap Pro. This popular, feature-rich imaging application offers an intuitive interface and powerful tools for capturing high-frame-rate videos and images across a wide range of planetary cameras. SharpCap Pro also offers advanced capabilities for image analysis, focusing, creating calibration frames, and more, making it a great choice for solar, lunar, and planetary imaging, as well as a wide range of other astrophotography applications.

SharpCap is available as either a feature-limited free version or a licensed Pro version. The Pro version is unlocked when you purchase a SharpCap Pro license from AstroSharp Ltd. and activate it within the software, effectively upgrading the free version to Pro. Please note that HelioMaker requires SharpCap Pro and is not compatible with the free version.
