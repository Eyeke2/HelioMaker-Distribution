Slew to Target and Safety

The Slew To Target button in the Dynamic Target Tracking dialog can command the mount to slew to the currently selected target. This helps with initial acquisition of the Sun, Moon, planets, or a loaded ephemeris target before final framing and focusing.

Before Slewing

  • Confirm that the selected target is correct.
  • Confirm that the observer location, time, and time zone are correct.
  • Check that the mount has been initialized and synced well enough for safe pointing.
  • Verify that the planned movement is safe for your pier, tripod, cables, telescope, camera, and filter equipment.
  • For solar work, follow all solar safety practices and keep safe filtering in place.

During Slew

Watch the mount while it moves. HelioMaker can request the slew, but it cannot know every physical constraint of a specific setup. Pier collision, cable tension, horizon limits, roof clearance, and meridian behavior remain the user's responsibility.

If the slew must be stopped immediately, use the available cancel/stop control for the slew operation. This sends a stop request to the mount so the slew can be interrupted in an emergency.

After Slew

After the slew completes, verify the target in the camera view or finder. Center and focus the target as needed before starting Dynamic Target Tracking, PHD2 calibration, guiding, or capture.

Canceling or stopping a slew is separate from stopping Dynamic Target Tracking. If DTT is already active and must be stopped, use Stop Dynamic Tracking in the DTT dialog.
