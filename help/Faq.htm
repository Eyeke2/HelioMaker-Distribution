Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This help section addresses some of the common issues that may arise during use of HelioMaker. Before consulting the rest of this chapter for specific questions or reaching customer support, ensure that you have carefully followed the steps outlined in the Installation and Prerequisites section. This includes verifying that all required components, such as SharpCap Pro and the customized version of PHD2, have been installed and configured correctly.

Additionally, confirm that you have activated your HelioMaker license as described in the License Activation chapter. Without a valid license, HelioMaker will not be fully functional.

Ensure you have also completed the steps in the Configuration and Setup chapter, including configuring SharpCap Pro and PHD2 for use with HelioMaker and verifying system connectivity. Proper configuration is essential for seamless operation of the software.

As a highly recommended step before using HelioMaker for real imaging, ensure you have completed the Checking System Configuration phase. This step verifies that all components are properly configured and operational, minimizing the risk of errors during your imaging session.

If all required steps have been completed and the issue persists, consult the specific FAQs below for additional troubleshooting tips.
