Installation and Prerequisites

Before installing and using HelioMaker, ensure that your system meets the following requirements and that all necessary software components are in place.

Safety Considerations for Solar Imaging and Viewing

  • Always follow established solar observing safety protocols. Ensure that the solar filter is securely attached before pointing the telescope at the Sun, and never look directly at the Sun without appropriate filtration.

System Requirements

  • Operating System:
    - Windows machines only (32-bit or 64-bit OS types).
    - Recommended OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 for optimal performance.
  • Computer Hardware Requirements:
    - RAM: Minimum 4 GB (8 GB or more recommended for better performance).
    - Storage:
    - For general usage: Any modern storage solution with adequate free space.
    - For solar imaging: An SSD with at least 1 TB of dedicated storage for image capture is strongly recommended.
    - Processor: A fast multi-core processor (Intel Core i5, AMD Ryzen 5, or better).
  • Equatorial Mount:
    - For best results, an equatorial mount with motorized drives on both the right ascension (RA) and declination (Dec) axes is strongly recommended. Although some users have experimented with PHD2 on alt-azimuth mounts, this can lead to reduced stability, field rotation artifacts, and inconsistent performance during extended sessions.
  • Imaging Equipment:
    - A suitable planetary imaging camera and telescope are required. When conducting solar imaging, the telescope must be equipped with a proper solar filter to protect both the equipment and the observer's eyesight.

Software Requirements

  • ASCOM Platform:
    The ASCOM platform provides a standardized interface for astronomy equipment, ensuring that various software tools like PHD2 and SharpCap can communicate reliably with your mount, imaging camera, and other devices. Installing ASCOM is essential for stable, integrated control and automation of your imaging setup. For more information and to download the platform, please visit the ASCOM official website.
  • ASCOM and Native Device Drivers:
    In addition to installing the ASCOM platform itself, you will need to install the appropriate ASCOM-compatible drivers for your mount, imaging camera, focuser, and any other essential equipment. These drivers ensure proper recognition, configuration, and control of your devices through the ASCOM interface. Consult your device manufacturer's documentation or website to obtain the latest drivers and follow their recommended installation procedures.
  • Camera Drivers for Direct Integration:
    For optimal performance, particularly in SharpCap, it is advisable to use the camera's native (direct) hardware driver rather than relying solely on ASCOM drivers. This generally provides improved performance, reduced latency, and better compatibility with advanced camera features. Before proceeding, verify that your chosen camera is supported natively by SharpCap by checking their list of directly supported cameras. Installing the native camera driver will help ensure smooth operation, faster frame rates, and more reliable imaging sessions.
  • SharpCap Pro:
    - A licensed copy of SharpCap Pro is required for HelioMaker and must be installed.
    - Version 4.1.12221.0 or later (with .NET 8) is necessary.
    - For download, installation, and purchase information, visit the official AstroSharp Ltd. website: SharpCap.
    - The free version of SharpCap is not compatible with HelioMaker.
  • Custom Version of PHD2:
    - HelioMaker requires a custom version of PHD2 (v2.6.14-solar.3.3 or later).
    - Download and install the custom PHD2 software from PHD2 for Solar, Lunar and Planetary Guiding.
    - For additional information about the custom PHD2 version, refer to the About the Custom Version of PHD2 section.
  • Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributables:
    - HelioMaker requires the Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 Redistributable files (x86, English version).
    - Although the HelioMaker installer assists in downloading and installing these files automatically, you can also download them manually from the official Microsoft website: Download VC++ Redistributables.
    - Note: The x86 version is required, even when installing HelioMaker on a 64-bit platform.

Other Recommendations:

  • Electronic Focuser:
    - While not strictly required, using an electronic focuser controlled by SharpCap Pro is recommended for more precise and convenient focusing.
    PHD2 and SharpCap Experience:
    - Familiarity and prior experience with both PHD2 and SharpCap are highly advised. Users should be prepared to perform some manual configurations to achieve optimal results. This may include selecting the appropriate camera and telescope in both applications, adjusting camera exposure and imaging formats in SharpCap, fine-tuning focus and framing, and configuring detection in HelioMaker's Find & Track tool. PHD2's local solar and planetary detector controls are used for standalone or fallback physical-camera operation. Selecting appropriate PHD2 guiding parameters remains necessary for stable and accurate tracking.

Licensing

  • License Purchase:
    - Choose from the available licensing options: Lifetime License or Yearly Plan.
    - After purchasing a license from the official website HelioMaker.com, you will receive an email with a download link for the software installer and your unique license key.
  • License Activation:
    - After installation, activate HelioMaker with the purchased license key.
    - Note: License activation requires an active internet connection and acceptance of the embedded End User License Agreement (EULA).
    - After activation, HelioMaker may prompt you to create a License Management PIN to protect future activation management tasks.

HelioMaker Installation Steps

  1. Download the HelioMaker Installer:
    - Use the download link provided in the email received after purchasing your license to download the ZIP file containing the installer package.
  2. Extract the ZIP File:
    - Use any standard archive tool (e.g., Windows Explorer, 7-Zip, WinRAR) to extract the contents of the ZIP file to a desired location on your system.
  3. Run the Installer:
    - Double-click the extracted setup file to launch the installation wizard.
    - Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.
  4. Install Prerequisite Software:
    - Ensure both SharpCap Pro and the custom PHD2 version are installed and properly configured.
  5. Activate the License:
    - Open HelioMaker and navigate to Help > Activate License to unlock the full version.
    - If prompted, create a License Management PIN and keep it in a safe place. You will need it later to review or deactivate activations from the License Activations Manager.

If you encounter any issues during the installation process, please refer to the Troubleshooting section or contact support via the official website.
